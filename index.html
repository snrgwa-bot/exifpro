Privacy by design

An app about privacy has to be private itself.

EXIF Pro reads and rewrites your photos entirely on your iPhone using Apple's ImageIO and Vision frameworks. There is no account, no advertising and no third-party SDK. Photos never leave the device. The app sends two things over the network: a version check to Apple, and, unless you switch it off in Settings, anonymous usage counts (which screens are used, how many photos were cleaned) tied to a random installation ID — never your name, location, device identifiers or anything from a photo.

FAQ

Good questions.

Does removing metadata change the picture itself?

No. EXIF Pro rewrites only the metadata containers. The image pixels are copied through untouched, so quality and resolution stay exactly the same.

What is the difference between Strip All and Secure Wipe?

Strip All removes the metadata blocks from the file. Secure Wipe first overwrites sensitive values (location, identity, dates) with zeros and then removes them, for cases where you want no trace of the original values.

Will it overwrite my original photos?

Not unless you ask. Save Copy writes a new photo to your library, and the app asks whether to keep or delete the original. You can set a default in Settings.

Does iPhone already remove location when I share?

Sometimes. Messages and some share targets strip GPS, but many apps, cloud drives, email attachments and marketplace uploads keep the full metadata. EXIF Pro lets you see and decide, instead of guessing.

Which formats are supported?

JPEG, HEIC and PNG photos from your library or imported files. RAW and video files can be inspected, and metadata editing support depends on the container.
