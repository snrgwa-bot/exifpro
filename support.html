Frequently asked questions

The app says it cannot access my photos.

Open iOS Settings › Apps › EXIF Pro › Photos and choose Full Access or Limited Access. With Limited Access, tap Import in the app to add more photos to the selection.

How do I remove the location from a photo?

Open the photo, tap Strip All (or choose the Anonymous export preset), then Save Copy or Share. To clean many photos at once, tap Select in the library, pick the photos, and choose Remove location in the bulk actions sheet.

Does Save Copy delete my original?

Only if you tell it to. After saving, the app asks whether to keep or delete the original. You can set a permanent choice in Settings › After Save or Export.

Strip All says metadata was removed, but the photo in my library still shows it.

Strip All changes the photo in the editor only. Nothing is written until you tap Save Copy or Share. The cleaned version is the new copy (or the shared file), not the original.

Why does the file size say "Unknown"?

For photos still in iCloud, the size appears once the original has been downloaded. Opening the photo triggers the download.

Which formats can be edited?

JPEG, HEIC and PNG are fully supported for reading and writing. Other formats may be read-only depending on their container.

How do I see the onboarding again?

Settings › Show Onboarding Again.

How can I delete all app data?

Delete the app from your Home Screen. EXIF Pro keeps no data anywhere else — there is no account and no server.

When you write to us, please include

  • Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version (Settings › General › About).
  • The EXIF Pro version (Settings › About EXIF Pro inside the app).
  • What you expected, what happened instead, and a screenshot if you can. Please do not send photos containing sensitive personal information.
