Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026 · Applies to EXIF Pro for iOS and iPadOS by Essential Dev Team

The short version. EXIF Pro does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data or your photos. All metadata reading, editing, stripping and scanning happens on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

1. Who we are

EXIF Pro is developed and published by Essential Dev Team ("we", "us"). You can reach us at essentialdevteam@gmail.com.

2. What the app does with your photos

EXIF Pro reads the metadata embedded in photos you choose (EXIF, TIFF, IPTC, GPS and related dictionaries), shows it to you, and can rewrite or remove it when you ask. This processing uses Apple's on-device frameworks (ImageIO, PhotoKit, Vision, MapKit) and never leaves your iPhone or iPad.

  • Photos are never uploaded to us or to any third party.
  • Edited or cleaned copies are written only where you direct them: back to your Photos library, or to the app you pick in the iOS share sheet.
  • The optional "Scan photos for sensitive content" feature uses Apple's Vision framework on your device to look for text and faces inside the picture. Nothing about the result is stored outside the app or sent anywhere.

3. Data we collect

None. We do not collect names, email addresses, identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports, location history, or the content of your photos. The app has no sign-up and no server of its own.

4. Permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhy it is requestedWhat happens with it
Photos (read & write)To show your library, read metadata, and save cleaned copies when you ask.Stays on device. You can grant access to only selected photos.
Location (while using)Only when you tap "Use current location" in the location editor to stamp a photo with where you are now.Written into that photo's metadata on device. Not stored by the app or sent to us.

You can change either permission at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security.

5. Network activity

EXIF Pro makes exactly one kind of network request: on launch it asks Apple's public App Store lookup service whether a newer version of the app is available, so we can suggest an update. This request contains the app's bundle identifier and is served by Apple; it does not include any personal data or photo content. If the request fails, the app simply continues offline. Maps shown in the location editor are provided by Apple Maps under Apple's privacy policy.

6. Data stored on your device

Your preferences (for example the original-photo policy, saved metadata templates and default author or copyright text) are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage and are included in your normal device backup. Deleting the app deletes this data.

6b. Purchases

Optional EXIF Pro purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The App checks your purchase status on your device using Apple's StoreKit; no purchase information is sent to us.

7. Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social SDKs. We do not sell, share or trade any data, because we do not have any.

8. Children

EXIF Pro is not directed at children under 13 and, because it collects no data, does not knowingly collect any information from children.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR, CCPA and similar laws you have rights to access, correct and delete personal data. Since EXIF Pro does not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to access or delete on your behalf; all app data lives on your device and can be removed by deleting the app. If you have a question about this, email us and we will help.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the effective date above, and describe the change in the App Store release notes.
